Shoplister Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Shoplister (“the app”) is a grocery list and home inventory app for iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • Your lists and library are stored on your device.
  • If you turn on the optional iCloud Sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud account. We cannot see that data.
  • If you share a household, the people in it can see and edit the shared lists.
  • We do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.

Information the app stores on your device

The app stores data locally so it can work offline. This may include:

  • Grocery item names, quantities, and organization (home and store sections)
  • Your shopping list and checked/unchecked state
  • App preferences (appearance, language, sorting, and similar settings)
  • Optional photos you choose to attach to library items
  • Backup files you export or import when you use those features

This information is kept in on-device storage (for example, app storage and, where applicable, a shared app group container used by the Shoplister share extension on the same device).

iCloud Sync and shared households (optional)

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings → Library. When it is on, the app uses Apple’s CloudKit to store your item library, shopping list, sections, saved lists, and item photos in a private area of your iCloud account, so they stay the same on all your devices. This data is kept by Apple under your Apple ID and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. We (the developer) cannot view or access it.

Sharing a household. If you create a shared household and invite people, the people you invite can view and edit your lists and item photos. Their changes are stored in your iCloud account and count toward your iCloud storage. If you join someone else’s household, the lists you work on are stored in the owner’s iCloud account, and the owner and other members can see what you add. Apple’s sharing screen may show people in the household each other’s names or contact details, as provided by iCloud.

Stopping. You can leave a household, remove people, or stop sharing at any time; the lists stay on each device but stop syncing. You can turn off iCloud Sync in the app, and you can delete Shoplister’s data from iCloud by managing your iCloud storage in the iOS Settings app.

Information we do not collect

Shoplister does not send your grocery data to us; we do not run our own servers. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email address, or phone number through normal app use
  • Use cross-app tracking or the App Tracking Transparency framework for advertising
  • Embed third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs

Camera and photo library

If you choose to add a photo to an item, the app requests access to your camera and/or photo library. Images are saved locally for that item. If iCloud Sync is on, they are also stored in your iCloud account (and shared with your household, if you share one). We never receive them.

Share extension

The optional share extension lets you add text to your shopping list from other apps. It reads a local catalog snapshot shared with the main app through Apple’s App Group mechanism on your device. That data is not sent to us.

Sharing you control

When you use system share sheets, export a backup, or send email to support, you choose what leaves your device through iOS. We do not receive that content unless you send it to us (for example, by contacting support).

Support email

If you contact us at support.shoplister@gmail.com, we receive the information you include in your message (such as your email address and message contents) so we can respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless needed to resolve your issue.

Children’s privacy

Shoplister is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support.shoplister@gmail.com
