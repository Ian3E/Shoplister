Shoplister Support

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Shoplister is a grocery list and home inventory app for iOS. Use this page for help getting started, troubleshooting, and contacting support.

Contact

Email: support.shoplister@gmail.com

You can also open Settings → Contact Support in the app to send a message with helpful device details included automatically.

Getting started

  • Home — browse your item library, search, and add things to your shopping list.
  • Store — shop your list by store section, check off items as you go.
  • Pull to add — on Store, pull down to quickly find or create items.
  • Saved lists — save a shopping list as a reusable recipe or list from the Store menu.
  • Backup — export or restore your library from Settings when you change devices.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Shoplister does not require an account and does not sync your lists to our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I clear checked items or the whole list?

On Store, open the ⋯ menu. You can clear checked items, clear the list, share the list, or save it for later.

How do I change language or appearance?

Open Settings from the Store tab. You can adjust text size, appearance, language, and more.

The share extension isn’t adding items.

Make sure Shoplister is installed and has been opened at least once, then try sharing plain text again. If something still fails, email support and mention which app you shared from.
