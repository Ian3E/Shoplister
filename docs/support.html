Shoplister Support

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Shoplister is a grocery list and home inventory app for iOS. Use this page for help getting started, troubleshooting, and contacting support.

Contact

Email: support.shoplister@gmail.com

You can also open Settings → Contact Support in the app to send a message with helpful device details included automatically.

Getting started

  • Shopping list — shop your list by store section, check off items as you go.
  • Add items — on the shopping list, tap + or pull down to quickly find or create items. Touch and hold + for more options.
  • All items — browse your full item library, search, and add things to your shopping list. Open it from the Add item screen or the + menu.
  • Saved lists — save a shopping list for later from the shopping list ⋯ menu, and reuse it from the + menu or All items.
  • iCloud Sync — keep your lists the same on all your devices. Turn it on in Settings → Library.
  • Shared household — share your lists with the people you shop with. With iCloud Sync on, tap Create shared household in Settings → Library and send an invite.
  • Backup — export or restore your library from Settings → Library.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, also in your own iCloud account (we cannot see it). Shoplister does not require an account and has no servers of its own. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I share my lists with my household?

Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings → Library, then tap Create shared household and invite people with Messages, Mail, or a link. They tap the invite on their iPhone and choose Join. Everyone in the household sees and edits the same library, shopping list, saved lists, and photos.

What happens to my own lists when I join a household?

They are replaced by the household’s lists. If you already have lists of your own, the invite offers Back Up First so you can save a copy before joining.

How do I leave a household, or stop sharing?

Members: Settings → Library → Leave Household. Owners: Manage shared household, then remove a person or tap Stop Sharing. Either way, the lists stay on each iPhone but stop syncing.

Why can’t I change the library language or clear the library?

In a shared household, only the owner can change the language, import a backup, or clear the library, because those change the lists for everyone.

Changes aren’t syncing.

Check that you are signed in to iCloud and online, and that Settings → Library shows iCloud Sync as Up to date. Opening Shoplister checks for changes right away. Items added from the share sheet or Shortcuts sync the next time Shoplister is opened.

How do I clear checked items or the whole list?

On the shopping list, open the ⋯ menu. You can clear checked items, clear the list, share the list, or save it for later.

How do I change language or appearance?

Tap the gear button on the shopping list to open Settings. You can adjust text size, appearance, language, and more.

The share extension isn’t adding items.

Make sure Shoplister is installed and has been opened at least once, then try sharing plain text again. If something still fails, email support and mention which app you shared from.
